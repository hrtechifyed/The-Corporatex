The role I joined and the role it became.

A demonstration of how a full story account will appear after contributor approval and human moderation.

01 · THE BEGINNING

The opening pull

I joined because the role promised meaningful ownership, a clear learning path and a team that appeared to value thoughtful work over constant urgency.

02 · THE GOOD PART

What genuinely worked

The colleagues were generous, the early projects were interesting and I learned quickly. This part of the experience mattered, even though the ending became difficult.

03 · THE SHIFT

When the experience changed

After a leadership change, priorities moved weekly and availability became an informal performance measure. The role remained described as flexible, but the day-to-day expectations changed.

04 · THE TIPPING POINT

Why leaving became necessary

The final trigger was not one dramatic event. It was the repeated gap between what was said and what the team was expected to absorb without discussion.

05 · THE CANDIDATE QUESTION

What I would ask before joining

Ask how priorities are changed, who decides what work is removed when new work is added, and how the company measures sustainable performance.

Story context

  • Anonymous contributor
  • Broad function: Product
  • Tenure: 3–5 years
  • Primary theme: Leadership & workload

Important context

This story reflects one person’s perspective during a particular role, team and period. It is not a definitive assessment of the entire organisation.
