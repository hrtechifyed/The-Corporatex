ABOUT

Why The Corporate Ex exists

The Corporate Ex gives former employees a structured, anonymous way to explain why they left and gives future candidates better questions to ask before joining. It is built around contributor control, clarity and responsible publication.

HOW IT WORKS

From private draft to published story

  1. 1. Add broad workplace context.
  2. 2. Answer guided prompts or write freely.
  3. 3. Review AI-organised suggestions.
  4. 4. Approve the exact wording sent to moderation.
  5. 5. A human moderator reviews the submission before publication.
🔒

PRIVACY & DATA HANDLING

Use only what the story needs

The platform uses contributor authentication, private draft data and approved story content to operate the service. Public experiences display an anonymous contributor ID rather than personal contact information.

  • Do not enter names, contact details or confidential employee records.
  • Drafts remain private until the contributor chooses to submit.
  • AI assists with organisation and privacy flags; it does not publish.
  • We do not sell contributor story information.

SAFETY & MODERATION

Care before publication

Moderation focuses on privacy, personal safety, responsible wording and usefulness to readers. Stories are contributor perspectives and are not presented as independently verified findings about an entire company.

No doxxingNo confidential recordsNo threats or harassment
