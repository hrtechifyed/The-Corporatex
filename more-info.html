MORE ABOUT THE CORPORATE EX

Exit intelligence,
not another review feed.

The Corporate Ex focuses on why people left, what changed and what a future candidate should investigate—not on creating another stream of anonymous reactions or a single employer score.

◎

ABOUT

Every company has exes. Their exits contain context.

A rating can indicate sentiment. A structured exit account can explain the promise, the experience, the shift and the question a candidate should ask before joining.

≠

HOW THIS DIFFERS

Conversations, reviews and exit stories serve different decisions.

  • Blind and Fishbowl centre professional conversations and community.
  • Glassdoor combines employer reviews, ratings, salaries, interviews and jobs.
  • The Corporate Ex focuses specifically on structured, contributor-approved exit stories.
✦

EMPLOYER CHOICE IN THE AI ERA

How an employer uses AI says something about how it values people.

Some organisations use AI to remove repetitive work, build capability and improve employee efficiency. Others may use the same technology mainly to increase output expectations or reduce headcount. Stories should describe what contributors directly experienced—not assume a company’s intent.
