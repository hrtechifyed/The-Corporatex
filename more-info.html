WHY IT EXISTS

Context changes decisions.

See the promise, the shift and the question to ask.

A structured exit account explains what attracted someone, what genuinely worked, what changed and what a future candidate should investigate.

2

HOW IT DIFFERS

Focused on the exit.

Not a rating, job board or discussion feed.

Blind and Fishbowl centre professional conversation. Glassdoor combines reviews, ratings, salaries, interviews and jobs. CorporateX centres structured exit stories and the questions a future candidate should ask.

3

AI ERA

How work changes matters.

Stories reveal how people experienced the transition.

Contributors can describe less repetitive work, new learning, redesigned roles, higher output expectations or reduced security—without assuming an employer’s intent.

4

TRUST MODEL

Perspective stays with the contributor.

Safety screening does not moderate opinions.

CorporateX screens only direct slurs, abusive attacks, threats or graphic violence, self-harm content and targeted abuse. It does not protect employers from criticism or rewrite a contributor’s meaning.
