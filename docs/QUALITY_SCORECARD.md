# The Corporate Ex — 9/10 Quality Programme

## Product standard

The platform should speak briefly, contributors should be allowed to speak fully, and every reader should leave with a better question.

## Six-phase delivery sequence

1. **Content reduction** — remove repeated explanations and enforce visible-copy budgets.
2. **Progressive disclosure** — reveal optional detail only when it becomes relevant.
3. **Trust and demonstration cleanup** — fictional examples, precise trust labels and moderation rules.
4. **Visual and interaction polish** — consistent typography, meaningful anime imagery and restrained motion.
5. **Accessibility and performance** — automated quality gates, keyboard journeys and performance budgets.
6. **Controlled-beta readiness** — contributor intake, moderation operations and genuine-story ingestion.

## Release gates

The site is not considered 9/10 until:

- the product is understandable within five seconds;
- Share Your Story presents only the Guided or Free-flow decision;
- users reach a writing field within two interactions;
- interface copy stays inside documented budgets;
- fictional examples never use real employer names;
- trust labels do not imply verification that has not occurred;
- user stories carry the depth while platform framing remains concise;
- keyboard, mobile, reduced-motion and build checks pass;
- publication remains a human decision;
- genuine beta stories have been reviewed and approved by their contributors.
