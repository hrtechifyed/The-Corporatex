# Contributor Invitation

## Invitation message

**Turn an exit into something useful.**

The Corporate Ex is preparing a small, invite-only beta for former employees who want to help future candidates understand what a job description cannot explain.

You can use a guided story or write freely. Your public account will use an anonymous identifier. Before anything is published, you will review the exact wording and a human moderator will check it for privacy, safety and usefulness.

We are looking for honest, balanced first-person experiences—not ratings, rumours or company-wide verdicts.

## Contributor expectations

- Do not include names or identifying details.
- Do not include confidential, customer or proprietary information.
- Describe only what you experienced, observed or were told.
- Keep relevant positive context as well as the reason you left.
- Explain what changed and what a future candidate should ask.
- You may request changes or withdrawal through the beta contact channel.
- Employee verification is not part of the first beta and no verification badge will be shown.

## Consent checkpoint

Before moderation, the contributor confirms that the account:

- is based on their own genuine experience;
- is not invented or deliberately exaggerated;
- removes identifying and confidential information;
- is a personal perspective rather than a universal claim;
- may influence another person’s career decision;
- is ready for human review.
