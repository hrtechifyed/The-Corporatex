# CareerJarvis experience contract

CareerJarvis is the original CorporateX guide derived from the human presence in the HRTechify identity. The character helps contributors turn a workplace experience into useful career intelligence.

## Product role

CareerJarvis guides transitions, explains privacy and safety, and then becomes still while a person writes. The character never judges an employer, scores an experience, rewrites a contributor's meaning, or acts out sensitive events.

## Journey

1. **Opening Signal** — Break Free, Next Act, Mixed Ending, or Pass the Torch.
2. **Set the Scene** — broad company, location, function, tenure, and arrangement.
3. **Story Beats** — eight focused moments with private Supabase saving.
4. **Final Cut** — contributor-controlled structure and wording.
5. **Release** — local safety screening followed by the existing protected moderation path.

## Motion hierarchy

- Ambient motion is limited to one slow signal or light effect in a viewport.
- Narrative motion appears only between major stages.
- Interface feedback uses short transform and opacity transitions.
- CareerJarvis remains still while an input or textarea is active.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` removes perspective and converts movement to immediate state changes.

## Dialogue rules

- One short line at a time.
- Plain meaning always accompanies cinematic vocabulary.
- No forced slang, jokes, celebration confetti, or trauma gamification.
- Positive, mixed, natural, and difficult exits receive equal support.

## Safety boundary

The active screen checks direct slurs, targeted abuse, threats or graphic violence, self-harm expressions, and basic identifying details. It does not send story content to an external AI provider and does not moderate employer criticism.
