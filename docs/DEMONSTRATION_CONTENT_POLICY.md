# Demonstration Content Policy

## Purpose

Demonstration content exists only to explain layout, navigation and moderation states before genuine contributor stories are available.

## Rules

- Use fictional employer names.
- Do not imitate a recognisable real employer, executive or current public controversy.
- State once, prominently, that the directory contains demonstration content.
- Do not repeat a warning chip on every row when one page-level notice is sufficient.
- Keep example stories balanced and plausible without presenting them as factual claims.
- Never mix demonstration and genuine stories without a clear visual and data-level distinction.
- Remove demonstration content from the default public directory once enough genuine moderated stories exist.

## Approved fictional employers

- Northstar Technologies
- Atlas Systems
- Meridian Group

These names are placeholders and should be checked again before public launch for accidental conflicts with active brands in the target market.

## Trust labels

Demonstration pages may use:

- Demonstration story
- Contributor perspective

They must not use:

- Verified employee
- Verified ex-employee
- Independently verified
- Confirmed account

## Transition to genuine content

When genuine stories are ready:

1. obtain contributor approval for the exact public wording;
2. complete human moderation;
3. assign the approved anonymous identifier;
4. remove or archive demonstration rows;
5. change the global line from **Exit stories. Smarter decisions.** to **Real stories. Smarter decisions.** only after genuine stories are visible.
