# Controlled Beta Playbook

## Objective

Prove that a former employee can turn an exit into a useful, contributor-approved and human-moderated story that helps a candidate ask a better question.

## Entry gate

The beta does not move to `ready_for_controlled_beta` until all of the following are true:

- at least 10 genuine moderated stories;
- target of 15 stories;
- at least 5 employers;
- at least 6 primary themes;
- a mixture of positive, mixed and difficult experiences;
- every contributor approved the exact public wording;
- every story passed the moderation rubric;
- no demonstration story is presented as genuine.

The current status is stored in `data/beta-status.json`.

## Contributor flow

1. Invite a trusted former employee.
2. Explain anonymity, moderation and withdrawal rights.
3. Collect the story through Guided or Free-flow mode.
4. Remove identifying and confidential information.
5. Preserve what worked as well as what changed.
6. Confirm the candidate question and optional right-fit caveat.
7. Obtain contributor approval for the exact final wording.
8. Complete human moderation.
9. Assign the anonymous `HRT-` identifier.
10. Add the approved record to `data/published-stories.json`.

## Initial beta group

- 15–20 contributors;
- 30–50 readers;
- 2–3 trained moderators;
- invite-only intake before open submissions.

## External dependency

Genuine stories cannot be fabricated by the product team. Phase 6 engineering can be completed before the content gate, but the beta cannot be declared content-ready until real contributors participate and approve publication.
