# Minimal Content System

## Principle

**The platform speaks in one sentence. The contributor speaks in full.**

User stories may be detailed because nuance belongs in the experience itself. Product explanations, navigation, forms and safety framing should stay brief by default.

## Visible-copy budgets

| Surface | Maximum default platform copy |
| --- | ---: |
| Home | 180 words |
| Share Your Story | 90 words |
| Guided Story | 140 words, excluding field labels and contributor text |
| Free-flow Story | 120 words, excluding field labels and contributor text |
| Stories directory | 80 words, excluding story rows |
| More | 140 words before expansion |
| Privacy & Safety | 160 words before expansion |
| Story detail | 80 words of platform framing; contributor story unrestricted |

## Section formula

A normal section should contain no more than:

1. one short eyebrow;
2. one headline;
3. one supporting sentence;
4. one primary action.

## Writing rules

- Explain an idea once.
- Prefer a verb over an explanatory paragraph.
- Keep headings concrete and human.
- Use progressive disclosure for policy and optional help.
- Do not use claims such as “real stories” before genuine moderated stories exist.
- Do not describe a theme cloud as data-driven until its sizes come from published outputs.
- Keep legal and safety meaning intact while shortening presentation.

## Review checklist

- Can the page purpose be understood in five seconds?
- Is there one dominant action in the viewport?
- Does any paragraph repeat an earlier idea?
- Can optional copy be hidden until requested?
- Is the platform speaking more than the contributor?
- Does every claim accurately reflect the current product state?
