WHY THIS MATTERS NOW

Employer choices carry more weight in the AI era.

As AI changes work, choosing how an employer treats people through that change matters more than ever.

Some organisations use AI to remove repetitive work, improve employee efficiency and build new capability—not simply to profit by reducing headcount. Exit stories can help candidates understand the difference.
