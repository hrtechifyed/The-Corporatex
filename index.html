A SMALLER, MORE USEFUL LOOP

From one exit to a better question.

The product is designed as a publishing experience—not an endless feed, a popularity contest or a company score.

1

Tell the experience

Choose a guided story or write freely. Start with the chapter that feels closest to your turning point.

2

Review it with care

Keep the account first-person, remove identifying details and confirm that the experience is authentic.

3

Help someone decide

A future candidate can use the story to ask a sharper question before accepting a role.

WHY EMPLOYER CHOICE MATTERS NOW

AI is changing work. Employers are choosing how.

Some organisations use AI to remove repetitive work, improve employee capability and redesign roles responsibly. Others may use the same technology mainly to intensify output or reduce headcount. Exit stories can help candidates understand how an employer treats people through that change.
