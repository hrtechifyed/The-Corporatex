BEFORE YOU SHARE

Your story may shape someone’s next career move.

Please review these responsibilities:

  • Share only your own genuine, first-person experience.
  • Do not invent or deliberately exaggerate events.
  • Remove names and details that could identify another person.
  • Do not include confidential, customer or proprietary information.
  • Write as a personal perspective—not a verdict on the whole organisation.
  • Aim to be useful, fair and responsible.

Perspective, not a company verdict: published stories reflect one contributor’s experience in a particular role, team and period. They do not represent every employee or the organisation as a whole. Human moderation is required before publication.
