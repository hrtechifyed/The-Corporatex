PRIVACY & SAFETY

Your story.
Handled carefully.

Private drafts, responsible wording and human review.

1

PRIVATE DRAFTS

Not public by default.

The prototype keeps writing only on the open page.

Refreshing or leaving clears the current Guided or Free-flow draft. A production database will separate private account data from publishable story data.

2

REMOVE DETAILS

Protect people and information.

Leave out names, records and confidential material.
  • Names and identifying descriptions
  • Customer or employee records
  • Trade secrets, source code or proprietary documents
  • Threats, harassment or deliberately misleading claims
3

FIRST PERSON

Write what you experienced.

One perspective, not a universal company claim.

Use wording such as “I experienced,” “I was told,” “In my team,” or “My understanding was.”

4

HUMAN REVIEW

Confirmation is not publication.

A reviewer checks safety, privacy and usefulness.

A future connected workflow will send confirmed stories for moderation before anything can be published.

5

AI BOUNDARIES

Organise, never invent.

AI cannot add events, decide truth or publish.

AI may suggest structure, detect possible personal information and reduce repetition. It must not intensify accusations or create facts.

6

CONTACT

Raise a concern.

Privacy or moderation requests are welcome.
