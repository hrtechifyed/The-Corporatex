PRIVACY & SAFETY

Private where it should be.
Public only after review.

CorporateX separates account identity, private reading activity and contributor records from the moderated workplace content that can become public.

1

ACCOUNT & ACCESS

Your email is for private access.

Readers can browse without an account.

CorporateX uses email-based authentication when you save or follow stories, ask a follow-up question, contribute a workplace story, or open My Space. Your email is used to identify your private account and deliver access links. It is not displayed with a published workplace story, saved-story list, follow, question or response.

2

MY SPACE

Saved and Following are private.

Your reading shortlist is not a public profile.

When you save or follow a story, CorporateX stores the account identifier, the story identifier and a timestamp so the item can appear in My Space. Those relationships are not included in public story views and are not shown to other readers.

Database access controls are designed so authenticated users can read and change only their own Saved and Following records. Authorized backend administrators may access operational data where necessary for security, support or system maintenance.

3

SUBMISSIONS

Your draft and public story are separate.

Submitting does not make a story public.

CorporateX stores contributor-approved Story Beats, broad workplace context, moderation status and related private submission records after an authenticated submission is created. New submissions enter moderation rather than publishing automatically.

Only moderator-approved public fields are exposed through the published story view. Private email, original private records and moderator-only data are excluded from that public view.

4

FOLLOW-UP Q&A

Questions and answers are moderated.

No unrestricted comments or private DMs.

A signed-in reader may ask a follow-up question on a published story. The question is tied privately to the reader account and enters moderation before it can appear publicly. A contributor response is also tied privately to the responder account and must be moderated before publication.

Public Q&A contains only approved question and response text plus public timestamps. Private asker/responder account identifiers are not exposed in the public Q&A view.

5

SAFETY SCREEN

Safety checks are narrow, not truth scoring.

Criticism is not removed merely for being negative.

CorporateX checks for basic contact-detail indicators such as email addresses, phone numbers and web links, along with a narrow set of direct slurs, targeted abusive terms, threats, violent expressions and self-harm expressions. These checks do not establish truth, detect every identifying detail, replace human moderation or score employer sentiment.

6

WHAT TO LEAVE OUT

Protect people and confidential information.

Share the experience, not private records.
  • Names or unnecessary identifying descriptions
  • Private email addresses, phone numbers or account details
  • Customer, employee, medical, payroll or disciplinary records
  • Trade secrets, source code or proprietary documents
  • Material you do not have the right to share
7

SERVICE BOUNDARIES

Where the product runs.

The website and the private data layer are separated.

GitHub Pages serves the public CorporateX frontend. Supabase provides authentication, database storage and trusted backend functions used by the GitHub Pages experience. Authentication emails are delivered through HRTechify's configured email provider. Render is used for testing/staging and is not the normal user-facing production destination.

8

RETENTION & REMOVAL

We do not invent a deletion schedule.

Current product behavior is stated as it exists.

CorporateX does not currently publish an automatic retention period for account records, Saved/Following relationships, submissions or moderated Q&A. Records may remain while needed to operate the service, preserve moderation history, respond to safety issues or support an active account.

To request account-data review, correction, removal or withdrawal of a contribution, contact hrtechifyed@gmail.com. Requests may require verification that you control the relevant account.

9

YOUR CONTROLS

Save, unfollow, withdraw, report.

Private actions should be reversible where the product supports them.

You can remove a story from Saved or Following from the story controls/My Space. Contributors can track submission status privately. Readers can report published content for review. For account-level deletion, correction or a withdrawal request that is not available directly in the interface, contact HRTechify.

10

POLICY SET

Privacy works with community rules.

Data handling and content behavior are separate responsibilities.

Read the Community Guidelines for content and conversation rules, and the Terms for account, submission and platform-use expectations.

This page describes the current product design and handling practices. Formal legal/privacy terms should be reviewed by qualified counsel before a broad public launch.
