PRIVACY & SAFETY

Private where it should be.
Public only after review.

CorporateX separates account identity and private activity from the moderated workplace content that can become public.

Your email is for private access.

Saved and Following are private.

Readers can browse without an account. CorporateX uses email-and-password authentication for My Space and contributor features. Supabase Auth handles account credentials. Your email is not displayed with published stories, saves, follows, questions or responses.

Saved and Following are private relationships protected by account access controls.

Your private submission and public story are separate.

Submitting does not publish automatically.

CorporateX stores the chosen ending, Story Beats, broad workplace context and moderation status after submission. Only moderator-approved public fields appear in Stories. Private email, private source records and moderator-only data are excluded from public story output.

Emails explain why they were sent.

Account and moderation updates stay private.

A new account may receive a CorporateX email to confirm the address entered. It is an account-confirmation step, not a magic-link sign-in flow. Future sign-in uses email and password.

After submission, the contributor may receive a receipt and HRTechify receives a review notification. If moderation approves publication, CorporateX sends the contributor a publication email. Full private Story Beat text is not intentionally copied into those notification emails.

Questions and answers are moderated.

No unrestricted comments or private DMs.

Questions and contributor responses are privately tied to accounts and require moderation before publication. Public Q&A excludes private account identifiers.

Safety checks are narrow, not truth scoring.

Protect people and confidential information.

CorporateX checks for basic contact-detail indicators and a narrow set of direct slurs, targeted abuse, threats, violent expressions and self-harm expressions. The safety screen does not defend employers from criticism, score whether a story is positive or negative, or replace review.

GitHub Pages serves the public CorporateX frontend. Supabase provides authentication, database storage and trusted backend functions. CorporateX does not currently publish an automatic retention period for account records, Saved/Following, submissions or moderated Q&A.

Save, unfollow, reset, delete, report.

Your account controls stay in My Space.

You can remove Saved or Following relationships, track submissions in My Space, recover your password, and permanently delete a story you own. Story deletion requires confirmation because deleted data cannot be retrieved.

For account-data review, correction, removal or withdrawal requests beyond the self-service controls in My Space, contact hrtechifyed@gmail.com.
