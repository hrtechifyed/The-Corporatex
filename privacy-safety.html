PRIVACY & SAFETY

Private where it should be.
Public only after review.

CorporateX separates account identity, private reading activity and contributor records from the moderated workplace content that can become public.

1

ACCOUNT & MY SPACE

Your email is for private access.

Saved and Following are private.

Readers can browse without an account. CorporateX uses email-based authentication when you save or follow stories, ask a follow-up question, contribute a workplace story, or open My Space. Your email is used to identify your private account and deliver access links; it is not displayed with a published workplace story, saved-story list, follow, question or response.

Saved and Following store the private account identifier, the relevant published story identifier and a timestamp so the relationship can appear in My Space. Those relationships are not shown to other readers or included in public story output. Database access controls are designed so authenticated users can read and change only their own Saved and Following records.

2

SUBMISSIONS & PUBLICATION

Your draft and public story are separate.

Submitting does not make a story public.

CorporateX stores contributor-approved Story Beats, broad workplace context, moderation status and related private submission records after an authenticated submission is created. New submissions enter review rather than publishing automatically.

Only moderator-approved public fields are exposed through the published story view. Private email, original private records, Saved/Following relationships and moderator-only data are excluded from that public view.

3

MODERATED FOLLOW-UP Q&A

Questions and answers are moderated.

No unrestricted comments or private DMs.

A signed-in reader may ask a follow-up question on a published story. The question is privately tied to the reader account and enters moderation before it can appear publicly. A contributor response is privately tied to the responder account and must also be moderated before publication.

Public Q&A contains only approved question/response text and public timestamps. Private asker/responder account identifiers are not exposed in the public Q&A view, and contributors are not required to answer a follow-up.

4

SAFETY SCREEN ONLY

Safety checks are narrow, not truth scoring.

Protect people and confidential information.

CorporateX checks for basic contact-detail indicators such as email addresses, phone numbers and web links, along with a narrow set of direct slurs, targeted abusive terms, threats, violent expressions and self-harm expressions. These checks do not establish truth, detect every identifying detail or replace review.

The safety screen does not defend employers from criticism, score whether a story is positive or negative, or remove a perspective merely because it is uncomfortable. Contributors should still leave out names, unnecessary identifying descriptions, private contact/account details, confidential customer/employee records, medical/payroll/disciplinary information, trade secrets, source code and material they do not have the right to share.

5

SERVICE & RETENTION BOUNDARIES

Know where data lives and what we promise.

The frontend and private data layer are separated.

GitHub Pages serves the public CorporateX frontend. Supabase provides authentication, database storage and trusted backend functions used by the GitHub Pages experience. Authentication emails are delivered through HRTechify's configured email provider. Render is used for testing/staging and is not the normal user-facing production destination.

CorporateX does not currently publish an automatic retention period for account records, Saved/Following relationships, submissions or moderated Q&A. Records may remain while needed to operate the service, preserve moderation history, respond to safety issues or support an active account. Authorized backend administrators may access operational data where necessary for security, support or system maintenance.

6

YOUR CONTROLS & POLICY SET

Save, unfollow, withdraw, report.

Private actions should be reversible where supported.

You can remove a story from Saved or Following from story controls/My Space. Contributors can track submission status privately, and readers can report published content for review.

To request account-data review, correction, removal or withdrawal of a contribution when a control is not available directly in the interface, contact hrtechifyed@gmail.com. Requests may require verification that you control the relevant account.

Read the Community Guidelines for content and conversation rules and the Terms for account, submission and platform-use expectations. This page describes current product behavior; formal legal/privacy terms should be reviewed by qualified counsel before broad public launch.
