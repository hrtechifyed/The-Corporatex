DATA PRIVACY & SAFETY

Serious stories deserve
a steady hand.

The product is designed to protect contributor identity, keep drafts private, remove identifying details and make publication a human-reviewed decision.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

Drafts are not public stories.

In the current prototype, drafts remain in the contributor’s browser. In the production release, private account data and publishable story data will be separated by design.

DO NOT INCLUDE

Protect people and confidential information.

  • Names, personal contact details or identifying descriptions
  • Customer or employee records
  • Trade secrets, source code or proprietary documents
  • Threats, harassment or deliberately misleading claims

PERSPECTIVE LANGUAGE

Write what you experienced.

Use first-person wording such as “I experienced,” “I was told,” “In my team,” or “My understanding was.” Avoid turning one experience into a universal claim about every person or team.

HUMAN REVIEW

Approval is not automatic publication.

Contributor confirmation sends a story for moderation. A human reviewer checks privacy, safety, usefulness and responsible wording before anything can be published.

AI BOUNDARIES

AI may organise. It may not invent.

AI assistance can suggest structure, detect possible personal information and reduce repetition. It must not add events, intensify accusations, decide truth or publish a story.

CONTACT

Raise a privacy or moderation concern.

Email hrtechifyed@gmail.com. Formal legal terms and jurisdiction-specific policies should be reviewed by qualified counsel before public launch.
