PRIVACY & SAFETY

Your story.
Your perspective.

Private drafts, contributor control and a narrow safety screen.

1

PRIVATE DRAFTS

Not public by default.

The prototype keeps writing only on the open page.

Refreshing or leaving clears the current Guided Story draft. A production database will clearly separate private drafts from approved public story content.

2

REMOVE DETAILS

Protect people and information.

Leave out names, records and confidential material.
  • Names and identifying descriptions
  • Customer or employee records
  • Trade secrets, source code or proprietary documents
  • Private contact, medical, payroll or disciplinary information
3

FIRST PERSON

Write what you experienced.

One perspective, not a universal company claim.

Use wording such as “I experienced,” “I was told,” “In my team,” or “My understanding was.” CorporateX does not require a positive or negative conclusion.

4

SAFETY SCREEN ONLY

Your opinion is not moderated.

The screen targets harmful wording and graphic content.

CorporateX checks only for direct racial slurs, abusive slang or targeted attacks, threats or graphic depictions of violence, and self-harm content. Contributors may still describe that discrimination, harassment, violence or abuse occurred without reproducing slurs or graphic details.

The screen does not defend employers from criticism, decide whether an experience is fair, rewrite the contributor’s meaning, or remove a story because it is negative.

5

AI BOUNDARIES

Organise, never invent.

AI cannot add events, decide truth or publish.

AI may suggest structure, flag possible personal information and reduce repetition. It must not intensify accusations, create facts, or change the contributor’s position.

6

CONTACT

Raise a concern.

Privacy and safety requests are welcome.
