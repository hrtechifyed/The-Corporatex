DATA PRIVACY & SAFETY RULES

Your story matters.
Your privacy matters too.

The Corporate Ex is designed for responsible workplace storytelling. These rules explain what information is used, what should never be submitted, how AI assists, and what happens before publication.

01

Privacy by design

Public stories use an anonymous contributor ID. The platform does not display your email address, profile photograph or personal contact details with a published experience.

  • Use broad role, region and tenure details.
  • Avoid precise job titles or unique project references.
  • Review all AI-organised text before approval.
02

Information used

We use the information needed to authenticate contributors, save private drafts, organise submitted text, support moderation and publish contributor-approved stories.

  • Email is used for secure sign-in and account access.
  • Draft content remains private until you approve submission.
  • Moderation records may be retained for safety and accountability.
03

Never include

Do not submit information that identifies or endangers another person, violates confidentiality, or exposes protected records.

  • Names, phone numbers, personal emails or home addresses.
  • Medical, payroll, disciplinary or performance records.
  • Client secrets, source code, internal documents or access credentials.
  • Threats, harassment, discriminatory abuse or doxxing.
04

How AI is used

AI may organise your account into clearer sections, identify possible personal details and suggest neutral wording. It must not invent events, intensify claims or decide whether your experience is true.

  • AI output remains private during review.
  • You can rewrite or remove every suggestion.
  • Your approval is required before moderation.
05

Human moderation

A human moderator checks approved submissions for privacy, safety, clarity and compliance with the community rules. Approval by a contributor does not guarantee publication.

  • Moderators may request changes.
  • Content may be rejected or withdrawn when unsafe.
  • Published stories remain personal perspectives, not verified company facts.
06

Contributor control

You control the wording sent for moderation. Contact HRTechify to request access, correction, withdrawal or deletion where applicable.

COMMUNITY SAFETY

Share experiences—not identities.

Be specific about workplace patterns and your own experience, but remove details that can identify colleagues, customers or vulnerable individuals. Use factual, first-person language and avoid presenting assumptions as proven facts.
