TERMS

Use the signal.
Respect the people.

CorporateX is built for personal workplace perspectives, private decision-making tools and moderated follow-up—not universal claims about an employer.

1

YOUR ACCOUNT

Use an email you control.

Your account protects private actions.

You are responsible for access links sent to your email account. Saved stories, follows, questions and submissions are associated privately with your authenticated account. Do not share access links or attempt to access another person's My Space.

2

SAVED & FOLLOWING

Your shortlist is for your use.

Saving or following does not endorse a claim.

Saved and Following are private organization features. A save or follow does not mean CorporateX verifies, endorses or agrees with a workplace story. CorporateX may remove or restrict access to content that is withdrawn, moderated, legally restricted or no longer available.

3

YOUR STORY

Submit your own experience.

Share only material you have the right to share.

Do not include individual names, confidential records, private personal information, trade secrets, source code or material intended to identify another person. Write from your own perspective and avoid presenting one account as proof of what every employee experienced.

4

PUBLICATION

Submission is not publication.

Stories and Q&A are moderated first.

CorporateX may hold, request changes to, reject, remove or withdraw content under its safety and community rules. A public story remains a contributor perspective rather than a verified company-wide fact. Follow-up questions and contributor responses also require moderation before they can appear publicly.

5

CONVERSATIONS

Ask useful follow-ups.

No harassment, doxxing or identity fishing.

Do not use follow-up questions to demand names, contact details, proof documents or clues that could identify a contributor or another person. Do not threaten, harass, coordinate attacks or repeatedly target a contributor. CorporateX does not provide unrestricted comments or private direct messaging.

6

PLATFORM LIMITS

Context, not professional advice.

Use multiple sources before making a decision.

CorporateX provides workplace context, not legal, financial, medical, employment-placement or investment advice. Readers should compare perspectives, ask appropriate questions and conduct their own research before acting.

7

DATA & PRIVACY

Private tools stay separate from public stories.

Read the current data map.

How CorporateX handles email access, Saved, Following, submissions, moderated Q&A and service providers is described in Privacy & Safety. Content rules are described in the Community Guidelines.

8
