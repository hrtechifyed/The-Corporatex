HOW TO USE CORPORATEX

Find the signal.
Use it forward.

CorporateX helps you learn from workplace exit stories, keep useful ones in your private space, follow moderated conversations and contribute your own experience safely.

Browse StoriesOpen My SpaceShare Your Story

FOR READERS

Use stories as context, not a score.

You can browse published stories without signing in. Sign in only when you want private account features such as Saved, Following or asking a moderated follow-up.

01 · DISCOVER

Browse workplace stories.

Open Stories and explore published experiences by company, context and signal. Each story is one contributor perspective—not a company-wide verdict.

02 · READ THE SEQUENCE

Look for what changed.

Pay attention to the beginning, what was promised, what worked, what shifted, why the contributor left and what they think a future candidate should ask.

03 · SAVE

Star useful stories.

Use Save on a published story when you want it in your private shortlist. Saved items appear only in your My Space account.

04 · FOLLOW

Keep up with a story.

Follow a story when you want to return to its moderated conversation or future updates. Following is private and is not a public popularity count.

MY SPACE

Your private CorporateX hub.

My Space keeps your reading activity and contributor activity together without turning them into a public profile.

★ SAVED

◎ FOLLOWING

✦ MY SUBMISSIONS

Your contributed stories.

PRIVATE ACCESS

Passwordless sign-in.

CorporateX emails a private access link. Readers do not need an account just to browse published stories.

SHARE YOUR STORY

Contribute the sequence, not identities.

The guided journey helps you structure one real workplace experience while keeping names and unnecessary identifying details out.

Set the Scene

Add broad company, location and optional team context. Do not add individual names, private contact details or confidential records.

Complete the Story Beats

Work through the eight moments in any useful order. You can answer, skip, go back and edit before the Final Cut.

Review the Final Cut

Read the whole sequence as another person will. Remove details that could unnecessarily identify an individual or expose confidential information.

Submit for review

Your submission does not publish automatically. CorporateX applies its safety checks and moderation process before anything can become public.

Keep control of the meaning. CorporateX structures the sequence; it should not invent, score or rewrite your opinion into a company rating.

FOLLOW-UP Q&A

Ask for context, not identity.

CorporateX uses moderated follow-up questions instead of an unrestricted comment section.

ASK

Ask a useful follow-up.

Good questions clarify role scope, team dynamics, workload, management patterns, growth, change or what a candidate should verify before joining.

AVOID

Do not identity-fish.

Do not ask for names, contact details, proof documents, private records or clues intended to identify the contributor or another individual.

MODERATION

Questions and answers are reviewed.

A follow-up does not become public immediately. Contributor responses are also moderated before publication.

OPTIONAL

Contributors can decline.

A contributor is never required to answer a follow-up question.
