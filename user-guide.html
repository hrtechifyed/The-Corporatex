SHARE YOUR STORY

Five short steps.

Contribute one real workplace sequence without exposing unnecessary identity details.

1. Choose the ending

Start with Break Free, Next Act, Mixed Ending or Pass the Torch.

2. Set the Scene

Add broad company, location and optional team context.

3. Complete Story Beats

Answer the moments that best explain what happened and what changed.

4. Review Final Cut

Remove names, contact details and confidential information before confirming.

5. Click here to submit

New contributors enter a private email and create a password of at least 10 characters. Existing contributors sign in with their account password.

What happens next. Your story enters private moderation. HRTechify receives a review notification and you receive a CorporateX submission receipt. If approved, the story appears in Stories and CorporateX emails you again.

PRIVACY & SAFETY

Know what stays private.

Your email, account activity and unapproved submission records are not public story fields.
